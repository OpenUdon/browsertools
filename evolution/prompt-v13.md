# Reviewed MFA And Typed Output Authoring

Replace inference at the live authoring boundary with exact human review.
Browsertools must accept only author-session v2, expose checkpoint-compatible
MFA kinds, and record the single kind actually exercised. Completion may add a
bounded list of current-observation accessibility outputs with safe keys,
scalar or presence types, and exact-name or proven unique-role locators.

The resulting private envelope must remain value-free, preserve
`goal_present`, sort reviewed outputs, and choose the oldest sufficient UWS
browser profile. Integer, number, and Boolean accessibility-text declarations
require browser 1.7/UWS 1.9 and Browserdriver protocol v3; downloads, cookies,
session transfer, goal inference, selectors, and credential values stay out of
scope.
